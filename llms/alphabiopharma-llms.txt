# Alpha Biopharma

> Alpha Biopharma is a clinical-stage biopharmaceutical company founded in 2017 and headquartered in Shanghai, China, focused on the clinical development and commercialization of global innovative drugs in oncology and immunotherapy. Its lead product, Zorifertinib (Zorifer, formerly AZD-3759), is a next-generation EGFR TKI approved by China's NMPA in November 2024 for EGFR mutation positive advanced NSCLC with central nervous system metastases. The company is backed by Qiming Venture Partners.
